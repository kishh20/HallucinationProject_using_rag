INSTITUTIONAL POLICY: CODE OF STUDENT ACADEMIC INTEGRITY & EXAMINATION CONDUCT
Document Reference: ETH-2025-04
Issuing Body: Office of the Dean of Academic Affairs & Board of Discipline
Applicability: Campus-Wide Examinations, Term Papers, Capstone Assessments, and Laboratory Evaluations

SECTION 1: EXAMINATION HALL ENTRY PROTOCOLS & TIMINGS
1.1 Reporting Time:
Candidates must report to their assigned examination hall at least 15 minutes prior to the officially published start time.

1.2 Late Arrival Rule (The 30-Minute Grace Window):
- Candidates arriving within 30 minutes of the scheduled examination commencement may be admitted at the invigilator's discretion. No compensatory or additional time will be provided.
- Any candidate arriving after 30 minutes from the commencement time shall be strictly barred from entering the examination hall, and their attendance marked as 'AB' (Absent).

1.3 Exit Restrictions:
- Candidates are strictly prohibited from leaving the examination hall during the first 60 minutes of the exam session.
- No candidate may leave the hall during the concluding 15 minutes of the examination to prevent disruption during the collection and accounting of answer booklets.

SECTION 2: PROHIBITED ITEMS & ELECTRONIC CONTRABAND
2.1 Banned Materials:
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, wireless earbuds, programmable calculators, portable storage media, and unauthorized handwritten or printed notes are strictly prohibited.
- Possession of prohibited electronic devices inside the examination venue, even in powered-off state, constitutes a presumptive violation of academic integrity.

SECTION 3: CLASSIFICATION OF ACADEMIC MALPRACTICE & DISCIPLINARY PENALTIES
Academic dishonesty and examination infractions are categorized into three severity tiers:

3.1 Category 1 Infraction (Possession without Use):
- Defined as: Being found in possession of unauthorized formula sheets, written notes, or unauthorized electronic gadgets without evidence of active copying.
- Penalty: Cancellation of the examination in the concerned subject, awarding of Grade 'F', and a mandatory formal written warning placed in the student's disciplinary dossier.

3.2 Category 2 Infraction (Active Collusion & Copying):
- Defined as: Active copying from illicit notes, exchanging materials or answers with other candidates, impersonation, or tampering with university barcodes on answer scripts.
- Penalty: Cancellation of ALL examinations registered in that semester, forfeiture of grades, suspension from academic activities for one full semester, and permanent notation on the official academic transcript.

3.3 Category 3 Infraction (Severe Breach & Paper Leakage):
- Defined as: Unauthorized retrieval, electronic transmission, or distribution of question papers, hacking exam databases, threatening invigilators, or bribery.
- Penalty: Immediate expulsion from the university and notification to relevant law enforcement agencies.

SECTION 4: TRANSPARENCY, SCRIPT INSPECTION & RE-EVALUATION POLICY
4.1 Answer Script Inspection:
Any student dissatisfied with their evaluation may apply for a digital certified photocopy of their evaluated answer script within 7 calendar days of the publication of semester results upon remitting an inspection fee of $25 per course.

4.2 Formal Grade Re-Evaluation:
- If discrepancy or grading variance is identified upon inspecting the script, the student may formally petition for independent re-evaluation by an external subject expert within 5 days of script receipt.
- A re-evaluation fee of $40 is payable. If the re-evaluated grade increases by more than 10% of the total maximum marks, the full re-evaluation fee shall be refunded to the student's institutional account, and the revised higher grade officially updated.
